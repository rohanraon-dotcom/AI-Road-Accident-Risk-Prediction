## Problem
Road accidents can occur due to heavy traffic, poor weather, low visibility, and risky road conditions. Existing navigation systems mainly provide routes and traffic information but do not provide a road-safety risk level.

## Solution

Our system analyzes traffic and weather conditions along a selected route to estimate road-safety risk. It identifies risky route sections and provides early warnings to the user.

## Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Leaflet.js
* **Backend:** Python, Flask
* **Database:** SQLite
* **Weather API:** Open-Meteo
* **Maps:** OpenStreetMap

## Setup
